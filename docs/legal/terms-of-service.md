## 2. What RECOSA does

RECOSA is a compliance co-pilot that helps SMEs understand and work towards compliance with EU regulations including GDPR, NIS2, and the EU AI Act. The platform provides:

- AI-powered compliance Q&A
- Gap assessments against EU regulatory obligations
- Document generation (privacy policies, data processing agreements, etc.)
- Website compliance audits
- Regulatory monitoring and alerts
