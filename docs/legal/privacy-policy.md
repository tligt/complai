# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 18 November 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how RECOSA collects, uses, and protects personal data when you use our website, our free compliance checks and our AI-powered regulatory compliance platform.

## 1. Who we are

RECOSA ("we", "us", "our") is an AI-powered regulatory compliance platform operated by a sole trader, based in Belgium.

Contact: contact@recosa.eu
Website: https://recosa.eu

## What data we collect and why

### 1. Account data

When you create an account, we collect your email address and password (hashed). We use this to authenticate you and provide access to the platform.

Legal basis: Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR)

### 2. Company profile data

When you set up your compliance profile, we collect your company name, sector, country, and company size. We use this to personalise your compliance experience and determine which regulations apply to you.

Legal basis: Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR)

### 3. Free compliance checks

When you use our free checks (the website check and the GDPR, NIS2 and AI Act Quick Checks), we record your answers and, for the website check, the address of the website checked. If you ask to receive your full result, we collect your email address and send it to you. If you tick the box, we also send you occasional updates on GDPR, NIS2 and the AI Act; you can unsubscribe at any time.

Legal basis: Performance of a contract at your request — sending the result you asked for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR); consent for the updates (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR)

### 4. Usage data

We collect data about how you use RECOSA — questions asked, documents generated, audits run, and feature usage. We use this to improve the platform and monitor for abuse.

Legal basis: Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) — specifically, improving our service and protecting it from misuse

### 5. Chat and document content

Questions you ask and documents you generate are stored to provide the service (conversation history, document access). We do not use your content to train AI models.

Legal basis: Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR)

### 6. Website analytics

We use privacy-friendly analytics (Umami) on recosa.eu, on the free checks and on the login page, to understand how visitors use them (page views, traffic sources, the steps taken). It sets no cookies and collects no personally identifiable information.

Legal basis: Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR)

### 7. Contact form submissions

If you contact us via the contact form, we collect your name, email, and message. We use this solely to respond to your enquiry.

Legal basis: Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR)

## Who we share data with

The RECOSA platform — application, database and AI knowledge base — runs on servers we operate ourselves, in France. We use the following sub-processors to deliver our service:

| Processor | Purpose | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Scaleway | Hosting of the servers we operate | France (Paris) |
| Mistral AI | AI language model for compliance answers | France (EU) |
| Brevo | Transactional email | France (EU) |
| Umami | Privacy-friendly analytics, without cookies | [CHECK — the location of your Umami Cloud plan] |

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

## How long we keep your data

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Account data | Until account deletion |
| Company profile | Until account deletion |
| Chat history | Until you delete it or close your account |
| Generated documents | Until you delete them or close your account |
| Free check results and email (if you do not create an account) | 24 months |
| Usage logs | 12 months rolling |
| Contact form submissions | 12 months |

## Your rights under GDPR

As an EU resident, you have the following rights:

- Right of access — request a copy of your personal data
- Right to rectification — correct inaccurate data
- Right to erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restriction — ask us to limit how we process your data
- Right to data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
- Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests
- Right to withdraw consent — at any time, for anything we do on the basis of your consent

To exercise any of these rights, email us at contact@recosa.eu. We will respond within 30 days.

If you believe we have not handled your data correctly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority:

- Belgium: Autorité de protection des données / Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit (APD/GBA) — dataprotectionauthority.be
- France: Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés (CNIL) — cnil.fr
- Netherlands: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (AP) — autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl
- Luxembourg: Commission nationale pour la protection des données (CNPD) — cnpd.public.lu

## Cookies

We use cookies to keep you logged in. Our analytics set no cookies. For full details, see our Cookie Policy.

## Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and role-based access controls. Our application, database and knowledge base run on servers we operate in France.

When you choose or change a password, we check that it has not appeared in a known data breach, using the Have I Been Pwned service. Only the first five characters of a one-way hash of the password are sent; your password, its full hash and your email address never leave our servers.

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of material changes by email. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.
