# Cookie Policy

*Last updated: 18 November 2026*

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help a website remember information about your visit — such as your login status — so you don't have to re-enter it every time.

## Our website (recosa.eu)

Our website sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device.

We measure visits with privacy-friendly analytics — Umami, and the built-in statistics of Framer, the service that hosts our website. Neither sets cookies, and neither collects personally identifiable information: we see page views, traffic sources and the steps taken, in aggregate.

Because nothing is stored on your device, there is nothing to consent to and no cookie banner.

## The RECOSA platform (app.recosa.eu)

The platform uses only what it needs to work. These cannot be turned off without breaking the service, so they need no consent.

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| recosa_sid | Keeps you logged in | Until you log out, 24 hours without use, or 7 days at most |
| _streamlit_xsrf | Security — protects against cross-site request forgery | Session |
| stActiveTheme (local storage) | Remembers the light or dark display theme | Until you clear it |

Legal basis: strictly necessary — no consent required (Article 5(3) ePrivacy Directive).

The free compliance checks and the login page use the same cookieless Umami analytics as the website.

## No advertising or tracking cookies

We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting cookies, or third-party tracking cookies. We do not share data with advertisers.

## Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser at any time:

- Chrome
- Firefox
- Safari
- Edge

Blocking the platform's cookies will prevent you from logging in to RECOSA.

For more information about how we handle your personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
